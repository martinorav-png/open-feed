yeah that's CW - continuous wave, basically morse code over a single carrier tone. pretty standard for relay devices in the 300-400 band. short burst = dot, long burst = dash, pretty simple once you know what you're listening for.

alphabet for reference:
A  .-       N  -.
B  -...     O  ---
C  -.-.     P  .--.
D  -..      Q  --.-
E  .         R  .-.
F  ..-.     S  ...
G  --.      T  -
H  ....     U  ..-
I  ..       V  ...-
J  .---     W  .--
K  -.-      X  -..-
L  .-..     Y  -.--
M  --       Z  --..

dot    = short tone
dash   = long tone  (3x dot)
              [ gap between elements  = 1x dot ]
              [ gap between letters   = 3x dot ]
              [ gap between words     = 7x dot ]
could be a test beacon, could be an auth handshake, could be something weirder. if it's coming from the same direction consistently it's probably not drift. worth logging what you hear and mapping it against the table.
