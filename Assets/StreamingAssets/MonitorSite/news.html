Witnesses Report Unidentified Figure Crossing Highway on Foot and Walking Through Parkside Grocery Car Park - Photo Submitted to Herald

Multiple onlookers describe the same figure moving through the car park without stopping. A photograph submitted by a witness has been reviewed by this publication. Police say no criminal activity is suspected.
By Staff Reporter Westfield Herald
Photograph submitted to the Westfield Herald by a witness who was driving past on Riverside Road. The image shows an unidentified figure in the Parkside Grocery car park at approximately 02:14 AM. Reproduced with permission. (submitted photograph - Westfield Herald)

Multiple witnesses have come forward to report an unidentified figure crossing the car park of Parkside Grocery on Riverside Road in the early hours of Wednesday morning. One witness, who was driving past on Riverside Road, photographed the figure through their car window and submitted the image to this publication. The photograph shows the figure moving through the lot, walking away, its back to the camera.

"I was driving home and I saw it cross the road right in front of me," the witness told the Westfield Herald. "I had to slow down. It didn't look at the car, didn't react at all, just walked straight across and into the car park. I pulled over further up the road and took the photo from my window. I couldn't explain the shape of it. I took the picture because I knew nobody would believe me if I didn't."

A second witness, who had been walking their dog along Riverside Road and asked not to be named, said they saw the same figure from further down the street. "I saw it cross from the other side. The way it was built - the shape of it - I knew straight away it wasn't right. I turned around and went home. I didn't sleep."

A third account was provided by a driver passing through the area who said they slowed when they saw the figure on the highway. "I thought it was someone in trouble. But the way it was walking - very slow, very straight - I just kept going. I don't know how to explain the shape of it. It was just wrong."

Westfield Police were contacted for comment Wednesday morning. A spokesperson confirmed the department had received reports from the area but said no active investigation had been opened. "At this time there is nothing to suggest any criminal activity took place at the location," the statement read. Officers have not attended the scene.

This is at least the third report of an unidentified individual in the Riverside district in the past four weeks. Previous reports, also describing a figure on foot near roads late at night, were similarly closed without investigation. Police have not confirmed whether the incidents are being treated as related.

UPDATE - 10:42 AM: Two further witnesses have contacted this publication since the story was published. Both describe observing the same figure, independently, on separate evenings in the past two weeks near the same stretch of Riverside Road. Neither had reported it to police.
UPDATE - 2:17 PM: The store manager at Parkside Grocery has declined to comment. Police have reiterated that no investigation is currently open.

This story is developing. Westfield Herald will update as more information becomes available.
